OurPlace Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 7, 2026

OurPlace is a game made by Truce Games, in which two people share a virtual home, look after a pet and play together. This policy explains what information we collect, who can see it, how long we keep it and how to delete it. Our Terms of Service also apply.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile. When you sign in with Google, Apple or Discord, we receive an account ID and the email address the provider shares, and, if the provider shares them, your name and a profile-picture link. We don't receive your password. We store these with your sign-in only; they aren't shown in the game or used for marketing. We also store your display name, your character's look, your link code (a random code that connects you to your partner and friends) and, if you set one, your anniversary date.
  • Shared content. What you create with your partner: messages, photos, drawings, Daily Snap photos and captions, answers to in-game questions, notes on gifts, sticky notes, your wish list and your home's furniture and decorations.
  • Social and gameplay. Your friends list and friend requests, who may visit your home, game results (for example puzzle times and scores), coins, items and purchases.
  • Technical and usage. Which screens you open and for how long, gameplay events, and error and crash reports (what failed, the screen you were on, app version, device model, operating-system version, memory size and graphics setting). Error reports don't include your messages, photos or anything you typed. If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token and a record of each notification we send (its type and time, not its text).
  • Support and feedback. What you write in a bug report or survey, your device and app details, and any screenshot you attach.

Birthday. Setup asks for your birthday to confirm you're at least 14. It is required to continue. Your birthday is saved only on your device, where the game also uses it for a birthday-themed feature. It isn't sent to us, and neither is your age.

Profile photo. A profile photo you choose stays on your device and isn't uploaded.

Usage data and error reports are sent directly to our own servers. We don't use third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we don't use this information for advertising.

Advertising

OurPlace doesn't show ads yet and doesn't include an advertising SDK. We plan to add in-game ads. Before they launch, we'll update this policy to explain what information advertising partners receive and the choices you have.

How we use information

  • to run OurPlace: sign you in, link you with your partner and friends, sync your home and deliver notifications;
  • to understand how the game is used and to find and fix problems;
  • to answer bug reports and support requests;
  • to keep OurPlace safe, enforce our terms and comply with the law.

We don't sell your information.

Who can see your information

  • Any player, through search. Players can find you by typing the start of your display name or your exact link code. A result shows your display name, link code, character look and an internal household ID, so they can send a friend request.
  • Accepted friends. Households you accept as friends can see your game results on shared leaderboards (display name with times, guesses or scores) and, if your visit setting allows, visit your home to see its rooms, furniture, pets, and members' display names and character looks. Visitors can't change anything and never see your photos, notes, drawings, messages, coins or wrapped gifts. You choose who may visit (all friends, best friends or nobody) under Bond › Friends.
  • Your linked partner. Everything in your shared home, including messages, photos, drawings, Daily Snaps, answers, notes and your wish list.
  • Our team. Authorized team members can access stored information to run and fix OurPlace. We review analytics, error reports, survey answers and bug reports routinely. We access household content (such as messages and photos) only when needed to investigate a report, fix a problem with your account or comply with the law.
  • Service providers, as described below.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and server functions, so it stores all account and household information on our behalf.
  • Apple and Google deliver push notifications. They receive your device's push token and each notification's short text.
  • Google, Apple and Discord handle sign-in and share the account details described above.
  • GitHub hosts our private bug tracker. A bug report's text, app and device details and your link code are posted there. An attached screenshot is stored in our file storage at an unlisted web address, so anyone who has that link can view it.
  • Cloudflare hosts truce.games and our team's internal dashboard, which shows usage and error information. Cloudflare keeps request logs for both.

These providers process information to provide their services to us and under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Deleting your account

Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account. If you can't use the app, email us.

What is deleted: your sign-in (including the email and name the provider shared), your profile, your messages, Daily Snaps, drawings, answers, survey answers, wish list, gift notes, sticky notes, push token, analytics and error reports. If you aren't linked with a partner, your whole home, including its photos and drawings, is deleted too.

What stays with your partner: if you're linked, the shared home, its furniture and pet stay with your partner. Shared plans such as a bucket list or calendar stay without your name. Gifts you sent stay without your note or name. Content your partner created stays, even if it shows you; email us to ask us to remove something specific.

What we keep:

  • a deletion record (your account ID, link code, household ID, dates and the list of files removed) to prove the deletion and finish any cleanup that failed;
  • bug reports in our tracker, which identify you by your link code;
  • if you were linked, copies of photos you added to your shared home's chat or memory jar may remain in storage;
  • short-lived backup copies held by our hosting provider, until they're replaced.

Removing your name doesn't make kept records anonymous: an account ID or link code can still connect them to you. Email us to ask us to remove a bug report, screenshot or stored photo.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers and encrypted in storage by our hosting provider. Each household's data is restricted so that only its members can read it, apart from what friends and search results can see. Messages and photos are not end-to-end encrypted, so authorized team members can technically access them, and do so only as described above. No system is perfectly secure.

Your choices

  • Access and correction: change your character, anniversary and other details in the app. To get a copy of your information or correct something you can't change in the app, email us.
  • Deletion: delete content in the app where it appears, or delete your account.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app's settings or your device settings.
  • Visits: choose who may visit your home under Bond › Friends.
  • Do Not Track: OurPlace and truce.games don't currently track you across other companies' apps or websites, so a "Do Not Track" signal doesn't change anything. We'll update this when ads launch.

Age requirement

OurPlace is for people 14 and older. Setup won't continue if the birthday entered is under 14. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 14, we'll delete it. If you're under 18, you can remove content you posted by deleting it in the app, deleting your account or emailing us. Removal covers what's stored in OurPlace, not copies others made, such as screenshots.

This website

truce.games doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Cloudflare, which hosts it, keeps basic request logs (such as IP address, page and time) for security. The invite page reads your invite code from the link on your device and doesn't send it to us.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For material changes, we'll also tell you in the app before they take effect.
