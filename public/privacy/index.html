OurPlace Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 7, 2026

OurPlace is made by Truce Games. It's a shared virtual home for two people — partners, friends, or family — where you keep a pet, decorate together, and stay in touch. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

Who can use OurPlace

You must be at least 14 years old to create an account. We ask for your birthday during setup only to check this — see below.

What we collect

  • Display name — the nickname shown to your partner. Not your real name unless you choose to use it.
  • Birthday (optional-in-purpose, required at setup) — used once, on your device, to confirm you're 14+. It is not sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Anniversary date (optional) — if you set one, it's stored with your account so it can be shown in-app (e.g. a calendar highlight).
  • Profile photo (optional) — stored only on your own device. It is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Sign-in identity — when you sign in with Google, Discord, or Apple, we receive the account ID needed to recognize you on future sign-ins and the email address that provider shares with us, and, if the provider shares them, your name and a link to your profile picture. They are stored with your sign-in only: we don't show them in the game or use them for marketing. We don't receive your password.
  • Your link code — a random code used to connect your account with your partner's.
  • Friend search — other OurPlace players can find you by typing the start of your display name or your exact link code. A search shows only your display name, your link code, your character's look and an internal household ID, so they can send you a friend request, which you accept or decline. Nothing else (your home, photos, notes or games) is visible to anyone outside your household, except the game results and home visits described in the next bullets.
  • Home visits — households you have accepted as friends can visit your home to look around: they see your room, its furniture, paint and lighting, your home's background, your pets' names and look, and your household members' display names and character looks. They cannot change anything, and they never see your photos, notes, wall drawings, messages, coins or wrapped gifts. You choose who may visit (all friends, only the friends you mark as best friends, or nobody) under Bond › Friends. Your best-friend list is private to your household, except that when only best friends may visit, the friends you marked will see that they can visit.
  • Game results shared with friends — after you finish a Daily Trail, your display name and solve time are shown on the leaderboard to everyone in a household that you or your partner have accepted as friends, and theirs to you. The same people can see how you did on the Daily Word (your display name, the number of guesses and a colour pattern with no letters) and on Snap Match (your display name, your time and your misses). Nothing else is shared, and removing a friend removes you from their view.
  • Shared household content — things you and your partner create together in the app: chat messages, photos you send each other, shared drawings, answers to in-app questions/games, the notes you attach to gifts, the questions and answers you write to each other in Ask Me Anything, the sticky notes you leave in your home, and your shop wish list (your partner can see it; friends can't).
  • Daily Snap photos — the photo and optional caption you choose to send for the Daily Snap game. They are stored privately for your household (only you and your linked partner can see them) and kept until you remove the snap in the app or delete your account. A 30-day automatic clean-up is planned; until it ships, snaps stay until removed.
  • Device push token (if you allow notifications) — lets us send you a push notification (e.g. "your partner is in your place"). We keep a record of when we sent each one and what kind it was (not its text), so we can see whether notifications help; it is deleted with your account.
  • Product-interaction analytics — which screens you use and for how long, and gameplay events (e.g. a minigame finished), tied to your account so we can understand how the game is used. This is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking, and we don't use any third-party analytics or ad SDK to collect it — it goes straight to our own server. It is kept for up to 2 years, and deleted with your account.
  • Error and crash reports — when something goes wrong in the app (an error, or the app closing unexpectedly), it sends us a short technical report: what failed and where in our code, which screen you were on, how long the app had been open, the app version, and your device model, operating system version, memory size and graphics quality setting. It is tied to your account so we can see how many players a problem affects. It never includes your messages, photos or anything you typed. It goes straight to our own server, is kept for up to 2 years, and is deleted with your account.
  • Survey answers (optional) — if you take an in-app survey, we store your answers with your account and app version so we can learn what players want. Only the OurPlace team sees them; your partner and friends don't.
  • Bug/feedback reports — if you use the in-app "Report a bug" form, we collect what you typed, your device/app info, and an optional screenshot.
We do not show ads, and we do not use any third-party advertising or tracking SDK. Nothing above is sold or shared with advertisers.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase — our hosting provider. All account and household data lives on Supabase's servers; they process it on our behalf and don't use it for their own purposes.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the OurPlace team's private dashboard. When a team member opens it, counts and summaries drawn from the analytics and error reports above (and the error reports themselves, including device details) pass through Cloudflare to their screen. Cloudflare doesn't store or use it for its own purposes.
  • GitHub — if you file an in-app bug or feedback report, its text and screenshot are posted to a private issue tracker on GitHub so our team can fix the problem. Reports are identified only by your random link code, not your name or account. Bug reports and their screenshots are kept for up to 12 months for support purposes and are not deleted when you delete your account (like a support ticket at any company). You can ask us to remove one by emailing us — see below.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your sign-in (including the email and name your sign-in provider shared) and personal data: your profile, your own chat messages, photos, Daily Snaps, drawings, answers, survey answers, your wish list, your analytics and error reports, the questions and answers you wrote in Ask Me Anything (including the ones your partner was part of), gift notes (a gift you sent stays in your partner's home without your note or name), and the sticky notes you left in your home.

If you're linked with a partner, your shared home and pet stay with them — deleting your account doesn't take away what you built together. Joint plans you both kept (like a shared bucket list or calendar) stay too, just without your name attached. Photos or drawings that only exist as part of your shared household stay with your partner for the same reason, and may still show you (for example, a photo of you); email us and we'll remove anything specific. Your bug/feedback reports on GitHub are kept per the retention note above. We also keep a minimal record that the deletion happened (your account id, link code and the date, plus a list of the files we removed) so we can prove it was carried out and finish any cleanup that failed part-way; nothing in that record identifies you by name.

To request deletion of data we haven't covered above, or if you can't access the app, email us at email.truce.games@gmail.com.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and household content (profile, messages, photos, Daily Snaps, drawings, answers, notes, wish list) — for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes it, with the exceptions listed under "Deleting your account".
  • Analytics and error reports — up to 2 years, then deleted automatically. Deleting your account removes them sooner.
  • Bug/feedback reports — up to 12 months (see "Who we share it with").
  • Connection logs — our hosting providers keep basic request logs (such as your IP address and the time of a request) for a short time to run and protect the service.
  • Hosting copies — our hosting provider may keep short-lived copies of the database for its own reliability. Deleted data can remain in those copies for a short time before they are replaced.

Keeping it safe

Everything the app sends is encrypted on the way to our servers, and our hosting provider stores it encrypted. Each household's data is locked so only its members can read it; friends only see what's described above. Only the OurPlace team can see analytics, error reports and bug reports, and only to run and fix the game. Messages and photos are not end-to-end encrypted: the team could technically open them, and only does so when needed to fix a problem with your account or when the law requires it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep yours safe.

Your choices

  • See or correct your information — you can change some details in the app (for example your character and your anniversary). To see a copy of what we hold about you, or to correct anything you can't change in the app, email us.
  • Notifications — turn them off in the app's settings or your phone's settings at any time.
  • Tracking and "Do Not Track" — we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't let anyone else do so through OurPlace or truce.games. Because there is no cross-site tracking to switch off, a browser's "Do Not Track" signal doesn't change anything here.

Players under 18

OurPlace is for people 14 and older and is not made for children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete it. If you're under 18 (including California residents, under California law), you can remove anything you posted: delete it in the app where it appears, delete your account, or email us and we'll remove it. Removal covers what's stored in OurPlace; it can't reach a copy someone else made, such as a screenshot.

Copyright

If you believe something in OurPlace uses your copyrighted work without permission, email us with a description of the work, where it appears, and your contact details. We'll review it and remove it if it infringes.

This website

truce.games uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which keeps basic request logs (such as your IP address, the page and the time) for a short time for security. The invite page reads your invite code from the link on your own device; it isn't sent to us.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll also tell you in the app before they take effect.

Questions

Email email.truce.games@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.
