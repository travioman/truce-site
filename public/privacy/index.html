OurPlace Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

OurPlace is made by Truce Games. It's a shared virtual home for two people — partners, friends, or family — where you keep a pet, decorate together, and stay in touch. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

Who can use OurPlace

You must be at least 14 years old to create an account. We ask for your birthday during setup only to check this — see below.

What we collect

  • Display name — the nickname shown to your partner. Not your real name unless you choose to use it.
  • Birthday (optional-in-purpose, required at setup) — used once, on your device, to confirm you're 14+. It is not sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Anniversary date (optional) — if you set one, it's stored with your account so it can be shown in-app (e.g. a calendar highlight).
  • Profile photo (optional) — stored only on your own device. It is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Sign-in identity — when you sign in with Google, Discord, or Apple, we receive the account ID needed to recognize you on future sign-ins. We don't receive your password.
  • Your link code — a random code used to connect your account with your partner's.
  • Friend search — other OurPlace players can find you by typing the start of your display name or your exact link code. A search shows only your display name, your link code, your character's look and an internal household ID, so they can send you a friend request, which you accept or decline. Nothing else (your home, photos, notes or games) is visible to anyone outside your household, except the game results and home visits described in the next bullets.
  • Home visits — households you have accepted as friends can visit your home to look around: they see your room, its furniture, paint and lighting, your home's background, your pets' names and look, and your household members' display names and character looks. They cannot change anything, and they never see your photos, notes, wall drawings, messages, coins or wrapped gifts. You choose who may visit (all friends, only the friends you mark as best friends, or nobody) under Bond › Friends. Your best-friend list is private to your household, except that when only best friends may visit, the friends you marked will see that they can visit.
  • Game results shared with friends — after you finish a Daily Trail, your display name and solve time are shown on the leaderboard to everyone in a household that you or your partner have accepted as friends, and theirs to you. The same people can see how you did on the Daily Word (your display name, the number of guesses and a colour pattern with no letters) and on Snap Match (your display name, your time and your misses). Nothing else is shared, and removing a friend removes you from their view.
  • Shared household content — things you and your partner create together in the app: chat messages, photos you send each other, shared drawings, answers to in-app questions/games, the notes you attach to gifts, the questions and answers you write to each other in Ask Me Anything, and the sticky notes you leave in your home.
  • Daily Snap photos — the photo and optional caption you choose to send for the Daily Snap game. They are stored privately for your household (only you and your linked partner can see them) and kept until you remove the snap in the app or delete your account. A 30-day automatic clean-up is planned; until it ships, snaps stay until removed.
  • Device push token (if you allow notifications) — lets us send you a push notification (e.g. "your partner is in your place").
  • Product-interaction analytics — which screens you use and for how long, and gameplay events (e.g. a minigame finished), tied to your account so we can understand how the game is used. This is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking, and we don't use any third-party analytics or ad SDK to collect it — it goes straight to our own server.
  • Bug/feedback reports — if you use the in-app "Report a bug" form, we collect what you typed, your device/app info, and an optional screenshot.
We do not show ads, and we do not use any third-party advertising or tracking SDK. Nothing above is sold or shared with advertisers.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase — our hosting provider. All account and household data lives on Supabase's servers; they process it on our behalf and don't use it for their own purposes.
  • GitHub — if you file an in-app bug or feedback report, its text and screenshot are posted to a private issue tracker on GitHub so our team can fix the problem. Reports are identified only by your random link code, not your name or account. Bug reports and their screenshots are kept for up to 12 months for support purposes and are not deleted when you delete your account (like a support ticket at any company). You can ask us to remove one by emailing us — see below.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your sign-in and personal data: your profile, your own chat messages, photos, Daily Snaps, drawings, answers, the questions and answers you wrote in Ask Me Anything (including the ones your partner was part of), gift notes (a gift you sent stays in your partner's home without your note or name), and the sticky notes you left in your home.

If you're linked with a partner, your shared home and pet stay with them — deleting your account doesn't take away what you built together. Joint plans you both kept (like a shared bucket list or calendar) stay too, just without your name attached. Photos or drawings that only exist as part of your shared household stay with your partner for the same reason. Your bug/feedback reports on GitHub are kept per the retention note above. We also keep a minimal record that the deletion happened (your account id, link code and the date, plus a list of the files we removed) so we can prove it was carried out and finish any cleanup that failed part-way; nothing in that record identifies you by name.

To request deletion of data we haven't covered above, or if you can't access the app, email us at email.truce.games@gmail.com.

Questions

Email email.truce.games@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.
