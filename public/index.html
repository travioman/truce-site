Terms of the truce

What we promise.

Every Truce game is built on four terms.

Article 01

Quality first

We'd rather ship one great game than three forgettable ones. Every build is played and tested by hand before it reaches you.

Article 02

Build in the open

Players see our games early and often, and what they tell us shapes what we make next.

Article 03

Fair to play

No pay-to-win and no loot boxes. Spending is always a choice, never the way to win.

Article 04

Games we'd play

We only make games we'd want to play ourselves, with the people we'd want to play them with.
