The studio

It's time to call a truce.

Truce Games is a small independent studio. We make cozy games you play with the people you care about, and we make them with players, not against them.

Why "Truce"

Two sides. One table.

Players and developers have spent years treating each other as the enemy. We think they belong on the same side, and that's what our logo is: a handshake inside a controller.

For players

Games that respect you.

No dark patterns and no pressure to pay. Spend only on things you'd want anyway, and never on time with your people.

For developers

Players who have your back.

Show the work early, listen to the people playing, and build the game they actually want.
