About us

It's time to call a truce.

Truce Games is a small indie studio. We make cozy games you play with the people you care about, and we make them with players, not against them.

01 / The idea

Same side. Finally.

Players and developers have spent years treating each other as the enemy. Developers make the games players want to play; players support the games they want made. Everything Truce Games ships is built to put the two on the same side.

WHAT WE WON'T DO

No tricks.

No loot boxes, no pay-to-win, no ads and no third-party tracking. Never a timer between you and the people you play with.

TRUCEour promise
WHAT WE WILL DO

Listen early.

Show the work, test every build by hand, and read every message players send us.

A small team that plays a lot.
