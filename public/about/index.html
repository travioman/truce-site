The studio

Developers and players. Same side.

Truce Games is an independent studio built on one idea: games are best when the people who make them and the people who play them are on the same team.

Why Truce

Calling a truce.

Too often, players feel games are built against them, and developers feel players are against them. Our logo is our answer: a handshake inside a controller. We make games for players, not against them, and to a standard we'd want as players ourselves.
