Delete your OurPlace account

Effective and last updated: November 8, 2026

You can delete your OurPlace account and its data at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted, and what is kept. The full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open OurPlace and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. If removing any stored file fails, our system retries it automatically. Deletion can't be undone.

Can't use the app?

Email email.truce.games@gmail.com with the subject Delete my OurPlace account. Send it from the email address linked to the Google, Apple or Discord account you sign in with, or include your display name and link code. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours, and we'll email you when the deletion is done.

To delete only some of your data (for example a bug report or a photo) without deleting your account, email us and tell us what you'd like removed.

What is deleted

  • your sign-in, including the email address and name your sign-in provider shared;
  • your profile, character and link code;
  • your messages, Daily Snaps, drawings, answers, notes, wish list, Partner Shop rewards and survey answers;
  • your coins, Jewels and items;
  • your push token, analytics and error reports.

If you aren't linked with a partner, your whole home is deleted too, including its photos and drawings.

If you're linked with a partner

  • The shared home, its furniture and pets stay with your partner.
  • Shared plans, such as a bucket list or calendar, stay without your name.
  • Gifts you sent stay in their home without your note or name.
  • Content your partner created stays, even if it shows you. Email us if you'd like something specific removed.

What we keep, and why

  • A deletion record (your account ID, link code, household ID, dates and the list of files removed), to show the deletion was carried out and to finish any cleanup that failed.
  • Bug reports you sent, which identify you by your link code, so we can keep fixing the problem. Email us to have them removed.
  • Photo files you added to a shared home's chat or memory jar, if you were linked, may remain in storage. Email us to have them removed.
  • Short-lived backup copies held by our hosting provider, until they're replaced.

These records aren't anonymous: an account ID or link code can still connect them to you.

Questions

Privacy questions and requests: email.truce.games@gmail.com.
