What you do together

Move in. Make it yours.

Everything in OurPlace is shared. What one of you changes, the other sees.

1

Share a home

A 3D home that belongs to both of you. Decorate it piece by piece and every change shows up for you both.

2

Raise a pet

Feed it, play with it and keep it happy. It's yours to look after together.

3

Play daily games

Quick puzzles, photo snaps and questions about each other. Play them to earn coins for your home.

4

Leave little surprises

Leave a sticky note around the house, wrap a gift or answer today's question for them to find.

5

Visit friends

Add friends, drop by their homes and see who solved today's puzzle fastest.

6

Free and fair

Free to play, with optional cosmetic purchases and in-game ads.

Never a paywall between you.

Nothing is ever locked behind a timer or a purchase when it comes to spending time with your person.
