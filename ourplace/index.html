OurPlace

A cozy little home you share with your person.

What it is

Link up with your partner or a friend and move into a shared 3D home. Decorate it together, look after a pet, earn coins from daily rituals and minigames, and visit each other's places.

Free to play

OurPlace is free. Optional purchases are cosmetic only. Nothing is ever locked behind a timer or a paywall when it comes to spending time with your person.
